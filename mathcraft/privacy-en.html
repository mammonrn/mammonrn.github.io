MathCraft Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Data stored by the app

MathCraft has no user accounts and does not send what you calculate to the developer's servers, and the app needs no internet connection to calculate. The following data stays on your device only:

  • Calculation history from every mode (general, fractions, percent, equations, geometry, units, tools)
  • Settings: language, theme and key-press vibration
  • Whether you have purchased ad removal

You can delete history from the History screen, or delete all data by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob, which may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to serve and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

In-app purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your card or payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at collecting personal data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.
